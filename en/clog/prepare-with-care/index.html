THE JOURNAL · EDITORIAL

Prepare for a sale by giving every investment a purpose.

Set priorities for design, maintenance, and presentation.

Understand the home before setting the plan

Review condition, disclosures, and timing, then identify issues affecting safety, maintenance, and first impressions. Qualified professionals should assess specialist matters.

Make the space easy to understand

Design and staging should clarify light, flow, and use. The right investment depends on the property, comparable homes, and budget; there is no fixed-return promise.

Keep the presentation faithful to the home

Photography, copy, and property facts should be accurate and consistent. Presenting strengths clearly and explaining details honestly helps build trust.
