THE JOURNAL · EDITORIAL

Before choosing a home, consider the life you want.

Start with work patterns, commuting, and household needs.

Start with an ordinary week

Imagine an ordinary working day: the commute, a quiet workspace, and the routines of everyone at home. Writing down these needs is often more useful than starting with finishes.

Consider education and community together

Verify school assignments and enrollment requirements, then explore libraries, parks, and community activities in person. Boundaries and policies can change; confirm them directly with the district.

Allow room for the next chapter

Career changes, a growing household, or a new business may change your space needs. Discuss several possible scenarios before deciding which tradeoffs matter today.
