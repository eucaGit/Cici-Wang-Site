THE JOURNAL · EDITORIAL

Why one address deserves more than one online estimate.

Condition, precise location, and comparable sales deserve a closer look.

Define what makes a comparable property

Homes in the same city can differ in size, land, ownership, maintenance, and location. Explain why each comparison is relevant before using its price.

Consider closed sales and current competition

Closed sales reflect earlier decisions; competing listings offer another perspective. Both require dates, status, and clear sources.

Use a range to support decisions

A useful value discussion explains its assumptions, comparable homes, and uncertainty. It is an agent’s market opinion, not a formal appraisal or guaranteed result.
